AuraHealth: first-aid guides for clinical review

AuraHealth is a free research prototype (simulation only, not a medical device). While someone waits for an ambulance, it shows these steps one at a time and reads them aloud, and it saves the time of each step for the doctor. The steps are fixed text written in plain English from the International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies (IFRC), International first aid, resuscitation and education guidelines 2020. No AI writes or changes them. For teenagers and adults. Babies and young children need different steps: tell the 112 operator the age. Medicine doses are left out on purpose (for example aspirin for chest pain). Ask the 112 operator. The emergency number shown is 112 (India).

Please check each step: is it correct, safe and clear for an ordinary person under stress? Tick OK, Change or Remove, and write the change in Comments. Anything missing that a bystander should do (or must not do), please add.
