## August, public information only

Company claims (meetaugust.ai, row 4): 100% on USMLE, "leads" on MedQA and MMLU medical subsets, rival models unnamed, no outside review stated.

Research findings (row 3): preprint on 400 vignettes with simulated patients. 81.8% top-1 diagnosis, 85.0% top-2, 95.8% referral accuracy. Authors say real-world validation is still open.

Direct observations: see docs/AUGUST_VS_AURA.md

Do not set the August figures against AuraHealth figures as if they were the same test.

## Unresolved questions

1. NMC telemedicine text: the NMC link gave 404 and a MoHFW copy timed out. Law-firm notes say AI platforms may not counsel patients or prescribe medicines, and may only support a doctor. This rests on secondary sources. Read the primary text.
2. DPDP commencement: MeitY page gave 403. Law-firm summaries say Rules notified 13 Nov 2025, consent-manager rule one year later (13 Nov 2026), most duties 18 months later (about 13 May 2027). One summary disagreed. Confirm in the Gazette.
3. 112 India: no page content read. Do not describe what 112 apps or SMS can do until read.
4. HIPAA page: not read. Low priority, US only.
5. CDSCO: does an AI chat that answers symptom questions and a first-aid guide count as a medical purpose? The guidance lists triage as one. Needs an adviser and possibly a CDSCO class query.
6. NVIDIA: is the Muse Glimmer endpoint covered by these trial terms or by model-specific terms? Is there a paid or enterprise route with no-training terms?
7. EU AI Act: page does not say where a health chatbot sits. Not needed for India phase.
8. Which NHS and WHO text, if any, is in the app. The app quotes NHS thresholds. WHO is non-commercial only.
9. Row 13 (Apple page): confirm what the brief meant by it. It does not cover app rules.
